# GnomeGuard Insurance — Claims FAQ

## Do I need a police report to file a claim?

Yes, but only for theft claims. If your Insured Gnome was stolen, you must
file a police report and include the report number when you submit your
claim. Vandalism and accidental breakage claims (Plus and Premier tiers)
do not require a police report; photo documentation is sufficient for
those claim types.

## How do I file a claim?

File through the GnomeGuard claims portal or by calling the claims line.
You'll need: your policy number, the date you discovered the loss or
damage, photos of the damage (for vandalism or breakage claims) or the
police report number (for theft claims), and a brief description of what
happened.

## How long do I have to report a loss?

Claims must be reported within 5 days of discovering the theft, damage, or
vandalism. Claims reported after 5 days may be denied unless the
policyholder can show a reasonable cause for the delay (for example, being
out of town).

## How long does a claim take to process?

Standard turnaround is 10 business days from the date all required
documentation is received. Premier tier claims are expedited to 3 business
days. Claims involving weather or water damage always go through adjuster
review regardless of tier, which can extend turnaround beyond these
standard windows.

## What if my gnome is only partially damaged, not destroyed?

GnomeGuard reimburses partial damage on a repair-cost basis: you'll be
reimbursed for the documented cost of repair (such as a professional
touch-up or resin patch), up to your tier's per-gnome coverage limit, minus
your deductible. If the repair cost would exceed the gnome's coverage
limit, GnomeGuard instead pays a total loss settlement, calculated the same
way as a stolen or destroyed gnome.

## Can I insure a gnome that already has damage?

No. GnomeGuard does not cover pre-existing damage. A gnome must be in good
condition (no cracks, missing pieces, or prior repairs) at the time it is
added to a policy. Adding a damaged gnome to a policy and later filing a
claim for that same damage is considered a false claim and can result in
policy cancellation.

## If multiple gnomes are stolen in one incident, is that one claim or several?

It's filed as one claim covering the incident, but each gnome is evaluated
and reimbursed individually against its own per-gnome coverage limit.
Deductibles do not stack: only one deductible applies per claim,
regardless of how many gnomes were involved in that single incident.

## What happens after I move to a new house?

You must update your Garden Location on file within 30 days of moving.
Claims for incidents occurring at an address that isn't your current
Garden Location on file, more than 30 days after a move, will be denied.

## Will filing a claim raise my premium?

A single claim in a 12-month policy period does not automatically raise
your premium at renewal. Two or more claims in a 12-month period may result
in a premium adjustment at renewal, communicated in writing at least 30
days before the renewal date.
