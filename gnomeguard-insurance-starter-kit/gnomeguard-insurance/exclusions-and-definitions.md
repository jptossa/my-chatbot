# GnomeGuard Insurance — Exclusions & Definitions

## Definitions

**Garden Gnome (the only item GnomeGuard insures):** A molded resin,
ceramic, or concrete ornamental figure depicting a gnome, at least 6 inches
tall, intended for permanent outdoor display in a garden or yard setting.
GnomeGuard policies cover garden gnomes only. Other lawn and garden
ornaments, including but not limited to flamingos, birdbaths, garden
trolls, wind chimes, and decorative signs, are not covered under any tier,
regardless of value or how they are described when a policy is purchased.

**Insured Gnome:** A Garden Gnome that has been individually listed on an
active GnomeGuard policy, with no pre-existing damage at the time it was
added.

**Garden Location:** The single residential address on file where a
policyholder's Insured Gnomes are kept. A policy covers gnomes only at the
Garden Location on file; a gnome temporarily moved off that property (for
example, taken to a friend's yard or a garden show) is not covered during
that time.

**Policy Period:** The 12-month period beginning on a policy's start date,
after which the policy renews (with any premium adjustments applied as
described in the *Claims FAQ*) unless canceled.

**Total Loss:** A claim outcome in which an Insured Gnome is destroyed,
unrecoverable, or damaged beyond economical repair, reimbursed at the
policy's per-gnome coverage limit (minus the deductible) rather than at a
repair-cost basis.

## What is always excluded, at every tier

- **Non-gnome items.** As defined above, only Garden Gnomes are insurable.
  A policyholder cannot add a flamingo, birdbath, or any other ornament to
  a GnomeGuard policy under any circumstance.
- **Intentional damage by the policyholder.** Damage a policyholder causes
  to their own gnome on purpose is never covered.
- **Lawn equipment damage caused by the policyholder.** Damage from the
  policyholder's own mower, trimmer, or other yard equipment is excluded,
  even though the same kind of accidental damage caused by, say, a
  neighbor's mower would be covered as accidental breakage under Plus or
  Premier.
- **Transit and shipping.** Loss or damage while a gnome is being shipped
  or transported (for example, mailed to a buyer or moved by a shipping
  company) is not covered unless a separate shipping insurance policy was
  purchased for that shipment.
- **Address mismatches.** Incidents at an address other than the Garden
  Location currently on file are excluded, including after a move that
  hasn't yet been reported (see the *Claims FAQ*'s 30-day update window).
- **Pre-existing damage.** A gnome with damage that existed before it was
  added to the policy is excluded from coverage for that damage.

## Tier-specific exclusions

- **Basic tier** additionally excludes accidental breakage and weather or
  water damage; only theft and vandalism are covered.
- **Plus tier** additionally excludes weather or water damage; theft,
  vandalism, and accidental breakage are covered.
- **Premier tier** has no tier-specific exclusions beyond the exclusions
  that apply at every tier, but weather and water damage claims are still
  subject to individual adjuster review rather than automatic approval.
