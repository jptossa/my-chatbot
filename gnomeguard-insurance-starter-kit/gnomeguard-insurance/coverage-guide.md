# GnomeGuard Insurance — Coverage Guide

This guide explains what GnomeGuard policies cover, in plain language, for
each of the three tiers described in the *Premium Tiers* document (Basic,
Plus, Premier). For definitions of terms used here (such as "Insured
Gnome" and "Garden Location"), see the *Exclusions & Definitions* document.

## Theft

All three tiers cover theft of an Insured Gnome from its listed Garden
Location. To be reimbursed for a theft claim, the policyholder must file a
police report and provide the report number when filing the claim (see the
*Claims FAQ* for the full filing process). Theft is the only claim type
that requires a police report; damage and vandalism claims do not.

## Vandalism

All three tiers cover intentional damage to an Insured Gnome caused by a
third party, such as graffiti, smashing, or defacement. Vandalism claims
require photo documentation of the damage but do not require a police
report, though policyholders are encouraged to file one for vandalism
causing more than $100 in damage.

## Accidental breakage

Plus and Premier tiers cover accidental breakage: damage to an Insured
Gnome caused by the policyholder's own household activity, such as a
dropped garden tool, a stray soccer ball, or a fallen tree branch during
normal weather. Basic tier does not include accidental breakage; a Basic
policyholder whose gnome is broken by a dropped rake would need to
self-fund the repair or replacement.

## Weather and water damage

Only Premier tier covers weather and water damage: harm to an Insured
Gnome from wind, hail, flooding, or freeze-thaw cracking. Because weather
events vary widely in severity and documentation, weather and water
damage claims are individually reviewed by a GnomeGuard adjuster rather
than processed automatically, even at the Premier tier. A policyholder
asking whether a specific storm is covered should expect that determination
to require adjuster review of the specific incident, not a yes/no answer
from a policy document alone.

## What is never covered, at any tier

- Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder
- Damage occurring during the policyholder's own lawn mowing or yard
  equipment use
- Loss or damage occurring in transit or shipping, unless separately
  insured
- Any item that does not meet the definition of "Garden Gnome" in the
  *Exclusions & Definitions* document (for example, flamingos, birdbaths,
  garden trolls, or decorative signs are never covered, at any tier)
