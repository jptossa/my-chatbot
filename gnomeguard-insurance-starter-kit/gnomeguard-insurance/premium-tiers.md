# GnomeGuard Insurance — Premium Tiers

GnomeGuard offers three coverage tiers for garden gnome insurance: **Basic**,
**Plus**, and **Premier**. Every policy is billed monthly by default; paying
annually applies a 10% discount to the total.

## Tier comparison

| | Basic | Plus | Premier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly premium | $7.99 | $14.99 | $24.99 |
| Annual premium (10% discount applied) | $86.29 | $161.89 | $269.89 |
| Deductible per claim | $50 | $25 | $10 |
| Coverage limit per gnome | $300 | $750 | $1,500 |
| Maximum gnomes covered | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Theft coverage | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Vandalism coverage | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Accidental breakage coverage | No | Yes | Yes |
| Weather and water damage coverage | No | No | Yes |
| Claims turnaround | Standard (10 business days) | Standard (10 business days) | Expedited (3 business days) |

## What "coverage limit per gnome" means

The coverage limit is the maximum GnomeGuard will reimburse for a single
insured gnome in one claim, before the deductible is subtracted. For example,
a Plus tier policyholder whose $600 garden gnome is stolen would receive
$600 minus the $25 deductible, or $575, since $600 is under the $750 Plus
tier limit. A Basic tier policyholder with the same $600 gnome would only
receive $300 minus the $50 deductible, or $250, because Basic's per-gnome
limit caps the payout at $300 regardless of the gnome's actual value.

## Upgrading or downgrading a tier

Policyholders can change tiers once per policy period (12 months) without a
fee. Upgrading mid-period prorates the difference in premium for the
remaining months. Downgrading mid-period does not generate a refund for the
current period; the lower premium takes effect at the next renewal.

## Adding gnomes to a policy

Each tier's "maximum gnomes covered" is the number of individually listed
gnomes a single policy can insure at that tier's per-gnome coverage limit.
Policyholders who own more gnomes than their tier allows must either upgrade
to a tier with a higher maximum or purchase a second policy for the
additional gnomes. Premier's unlimited maximum means no second policy is
ever needed at that tier.

## Multi-policy discount

Policyholders insuring gnomes at two or more separate garden locations (see
the *Exclusions & Definitions* document for the definition of a Garden
Location) receive a 5% discount on the second and any subsequent policy,
applied to that policy's premium only.
